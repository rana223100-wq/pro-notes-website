Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Pro Notes (“we,” “our,” or “the app”) handles information when you use the Pro Notes note-taking application.

Publisher review required: Before publishing this page, the app publisher must confirm that every statement below accurately reflects the app's actual code, services, analytics, advertising SDKs, and data practices. Edit this policy where necessary.

Information you create

Notes and attachments you create may be stored on your device. The exact data stored depends on the features you use and your app version. You are responsible for the content you choose to put in your notes.

Google account and Google Drive backups

If you choose to connect a Google account and use Google Drive backup, the app may access the Google Drive files permitted by the authorization you grant, for the purpose of creating or restoring app backups. Backup data is intended to be stored in the Google account you authorize. Google's handling of data is also subject to Google's own privacy policy and terms.

You can choose whether to use available account and backup features. Do not assume a backup has completed until the app confirms success. You should periodically check that your backup is available and restorable.

How information is used

Information is used to provide the app's note-taking, organization, security, backup, and restore features that you choose to use. Any additional uses—such as diagnostics, analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or account services—must be described here by the publisher if they are present in the app.

Storage, security, and retention

We recommend protecting your device and Google account with strong authentication. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. Local data may remain on your device until you delete it or uninstall/reset the app; cloud backup copies may remain in Google Drive until you delete them. Exact retention behavior depends on the app's implementation and your actions.

Sharing

We do not intend to sell the contents of your personal notes. The publisher must disclose any sharing with service providers or third parties used by the app, including cloud, analytics, crash reporting, and advertising providers, as applicable.

Children's privacy

The publisher should specify the intended age group and any applicable child-privacy practices before publication. Do not use the app in a way that violates applicable laws or platform requirements.

Your choices

You can choose whether to use optional Google Drive backup and can manage access through your Google Account security settings. You may delete notes in the app and manage backup files in Google Drive, subject to the app's available features.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. For privacy questions or requests, contact the app publisher using the contact details shown on the app's official Google Play listing.
